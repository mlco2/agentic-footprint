# Code of conduct

Agentic Footprint contributors are expected to keep project spaces welcoming,
constructive, and safe.

- Treat people with respect across differences in background and experience.
- Critique ideas and code, not individuals.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, and publication of private data.
- Assume good intent while accepting responsibility for impact.
- Respect maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and moderation.

Unacceptable behavior may be reported privately through the repository's
security or maintainer contact channels. Maintainers may edit or remove
content, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to
protect the community.
